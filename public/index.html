FAQ

§04
Does the team get tokens?
No. Zero for team, pre-sale and marketing.
Where does my payment go?
All of it into the locked pool. The team never receives it.
How does the team earn?
Trading fees from the pool. Not mint money.
What if it doesn't sell out?
Unsold tokens are burned and the 80:20 ratio between minted tokens and the pool is kept.
What if the pool can't be built?
If it isn't built within 7 days after the mint closes, every minter can reclaim exactly the HYPE they paid.
What do I pay with?
Native HYPE on Elysium, chain ID 99901.
